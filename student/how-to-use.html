You can create a Service listing for things such as programming, graphic design, photography, tutoring, repairs, printing, hairdressing and other student-oriented services.

Clearly describe the service.

Housing

Find accommodation

Looking for a place to stay? Browse accommodation listings posted for students.

Review the available accommodation information, including location, description, price and facilities where provided.

Be careful before making payments. Always verify the accommodation and the person offering it. Do not send money simply because a listing looks convincing.

If you have accommodation to offer

You can create an Accommodation listing and provide accurate information about the property, location, price, facilities and availability.

Lost & Found

Lost something? Found something?

CampusHub allows students to report found items and helps students claim items they believe belong to them.

If you found an item

Create a Lost & Found record and provide information such as the item name, description, location, date found, contact details and an optional image.

If you lost an item

Browse reported found items. If you find an item that appears to be yours, start the claim process.

Verification protects the owner Claiming an item may require you to answer verification questions about the item. This helps prevent someone else from falsely claiming your property.
Keep some identifying details private. If an item has unique characteristics, avoid publicly revealing every detail. Some information can be useful when verifying the rightful owner.

Trust

Verification & badges

Verification helps students understand which parts of another user's identity or activity have been verified.

ID Verified Student ID verification has been completed.
Hall Verified The student's hall information has been verified.
Top Seller Recognition based on selling activity.
Verified Indicates an applicable account verification status.

Why verification matters

Verification provides useful trust signals when you interact with another student. However, badges should be treated as one part of your overall judgment.

Security

Protect your account

CampusHub currently supports additional account security through Email and TOTP Two-Factor Authentication.

Two-Factor Authentication

2FA adds another layer of protection to your account. In addition to your password, you will need a second verification code when required.

Email 2FA

Receive your verification code through your registered email address.

TOTP 2FA

Use an authenticator application to generate verification codes.

1

Open your profile

Open the account/profile menu.

2

Select Add 2FA

Choose the available authentication method.

3

Complete setup

Verify the method and use it when CampusHub requests additional authentication.

Never share your verification code. CampusHub support will never need your password or one-time authentication code.

Changing your password

Use Change Password from your account menu whenever you need to update your password.

Safety

Stay safe on CampusHub

CampusHub connects students, but you should always use good judgment when interacting with other users.

Protect your account

Never share your password.
Never share your 2FA code.
Be careful with suspicious links.
Report suspicious activity.

When buying or selling

Review the listing carefully, ask questions when necessary and verify important information before completing a transaction.

When meeting another student

If you need to meet someone you don't know, choose an appropriate public location where possible and let someone you trust know where you're going when appropriate.

Report abuse

If you encounter suspicious, abusive or inappropriate behaviour, use Report Issue / Abuse.
