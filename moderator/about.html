Building Trust on Campus

CampusHub is a student‑only marketplace designed to eliminate scams, fake listings, and shady deals – so you can buy and sell with confidence.

The Problem

Students are constantly being scammed on campus – fake textbooks, stolen gadgets, ghost sellers, and dishonest buyers. Without a trusted system, every transaction is a gamble.

  • ❌ Scammers posing as students
  • ❌ Fake listings with doctored images
  • ❌ No accountability or recourse
  • ❌ Wasted time and money

The Solution

CampusHub verifies every user with student ID, tracks trust scores, and gives you the tools to spot red flags before you pay.

  • ✅ Verified student accounts
  • ✅ Trust scores based on real transactions
  • ✅ Secure messaging and dispute support
  • ✅ Honest reviews from real buyers
