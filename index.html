Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I trust CampusConnect?
Every user must verify with their university email and student ID. We also have hall verification and a reporting system to keep the community safe.
Is it free?
Yes! Browsing and posting listings is completely free. You only pay when you buy an item.
Can outsiders join?
No. Only current Demo University students with a valid @demo.edu.ng or @stu.demo.edu.ng email can join.
How do you verify students?
We send a verification link to your university email. You also have the option to upload a photo of your student ID.
Who handles payments?
CampusConnect does NOT handle payments. Buyers and sellers meet in person to inspect and pay directly.
What happens if someone scams me?
Report the user immediately using the “Report” button. Our moderation team will investigate and take action.
